## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

### Priority 1: Respond to the Landlord Resource Page Build (This Week)

**1. Build a standalone landlord compliance checklist page.**

Reedy published `reedyandcompany.com/landlord-compliance-checklist` on October 19. Advantage does not have an equivalent standalone compliance checklist page. Advantage has `/annual-inspections/`, `/what-tennessee-landlords-need-to-know-about-rental-property-inspections/`, and `/common-lease-violations/`, but none serve as a comprehensive, printable or scannable compliance checklist for Memphis and suburban Tennessee and Mississippi landlords. Build `/landlord-compliance-checklist/` or `/memphis-landlord-compliance-checklist/` as a standalone resource. Format it as a true checklist covering lease requirements, habitability standards, notice obligations, security deposit rules, and inspection protocols under Tennessee and Mississippi law. This is a high-intent owner acquisition asset.

**2. Expand the existing rent-ready content rather than building a duplicate.**

Advantage already has `/what-is-a-rent-ready-property-essential-guide-for-new-landlords/` and `/rent-ready-checklist-for-lakeland-landlords-the-exact-steps-before-listing/`. Reedy is targeting this same space with `reedyandcompany.com/rent-ready-turn-checklist`. Rather than building a net-new page, expand the existing Lakeland rent-ready checklist into a broader, market-wide version at a new URL such as `/rent-ready-turn-checklist-memphis-landlords/`. This directly competes with Reedy's asset on the same keyword intent while preserving Advantage's existing page equity.

### Priority 2: Counter the Arlington and Germantown Service Area Push

**3. Strengthen the existing Arlington and Germantown service area pages.**

Advantage already has `/property-management-arlington-tn/` and `/property-management-germantown-tn/`. Reedy just refreshed its equivalent pages on October 20. This is a direct competitive signal.
