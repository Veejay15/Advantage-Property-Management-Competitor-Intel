## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

**1. Strengthen the existing `/property-management-fees/` page immediately.**

Reedy just launched a dedicated `/pricing` page, directly targeting cost-comparison queries from owners evaluating companies. Advantage already has `/property-management-fees/` but this is the week to audit it. Make sure it clearly states fee structures, compares management models, and addresses common objections. Add a structured FAQ section targeting queries like "how much does property management cost in Memphis" and "what is included in a property management fee." Do not let Reedy own this SERP uncontested.

**2. Build a dedicated `/single-family-property-management/` page.**

Advantage has `/single-family-homes/` but that page reads as a property type description, not a targeted service page for owners of single-family rentals. Reedy's new `/single-family-property-management` page targets the owner audience explicitly. Build a new page (or restructure the existing one) around the service offering: what Advantage does for single-family rental owners, why it differs from multi-family management, and local Memphis-area context. Target: "single family home property management Memphis."

**3. Build a dedicated `/multi-family-property-management/` page.**

Advantage has `/duplexes-and-triplexes/` but no equivalent service page targeting multi-family owner keywords. Reedy now has both `/multi-family-property-management` and `/multi-family-rentals`. Build a service page targeting owners of duplexes, triplexes, and small apartment buildings in the Memphis market. This is a genuine content gap.

**4. Expand the existing `/property-management-germantown-tn/` page to outperform Reedy's new entry.**

Advantage already has this page. Reedy's `/germantown-property-management` page has the freshest lastmod date of the entire batch (2026-10-02), which signals it was a deliberate, prioritized build. Audit the Advantage Germantown page now. Add local rental market data, specific neighborhood references, owner FAQ content, and internal links from related blog posts and service pages. Do not allow Reedy to outrank you on one of the highest-income submarkets in the area.

**5. Build a `/landlord-compliance-checklist/` resource page.**

Advantage has no equivalent to Reedy's new `/landlord-compliance-checklist` page. This is a genuine gap. A compliance checklist page targeting Tennessee landlord requirements (habitability standards, notice requirements, security deposit rules, lead paint disclosures, MLGW-related obligations) serves dual purposes: it ranks for informational queries and converts owners who are overwhelmed by compliance into managed clients. This fits naturally alongside existing Advantage content on inspections and maintenance.

**6. Build separate `/owner-faq/` and `/tenant-faq/` pages distinct from the existing `/faq/` page.**

Advantage has a single `/faq/` page. Reedy now has audience-specific FAQ pages at `/owner-faqs` and `/resident-faqs`. A single combined FAQ page loses topical clarity for search engines and dilutes conversion intent. Build two separate audience-specific pages. The owner FAQ should address management fees, contract terms, eviction handling, maintenance responsibilities, and reporting. The tenant FAQ should address maintenance requests, rent payment, portal access, lease renewal, and security deposits. Cross-link them from the respective `/owners/` and `/tenants/` hub pages.

**7. Audit and update the existing `/investments/` or equivalent page to compete with Reedy's new `/investments` page.**

Advantage has strong investor-focused blog content but no single hub page with the slug or focus of an "investments" landing page. Review whether `/why-invest-in-memphis/` or another existing page can be repositioned as a primary investor hub, or build `/memphis-investment-property-management/` as a conversion-focused landing page targeting out-of-state and local investors evaluating property managers. Reedy's `/investments` page is new and likely thin. Move fast.

**8. Monitor Reedy's domain for ranking recovery signals next week.**

A site migration of this scale carries significant risk of ranking disruption, broken internal links, redirect issues, and crawl budget problems. Pull a fresh SEMrush snapshot on reedyandcompany.com next week. If they drop rankings during the migration window, that is an opportunity to aggressively promote Advantage content on the keywords they temporarily vacate.
