## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

Since Reedy & Company made no content moves this week, the following recommendations are proactive maintenance and gap-filling items based on Advantage's existing footprint rather than direct counter-moves.

1. **Audit and strengthen thin service area pages.** Pages like `/property-management-eads-tn/`, `/property-management-lakeland-tn/`, and `/property-management-millington-tn/` likely face competitive pressure from Reedy & Company's broader Memphis-area presence. Review word count, internal linking, and on-page signals for each. Add localized detail (vacancy rates, commute context, neighborhood investor notes) if those pages are running under 600 words.

If the current page is not already covering management rate ranges, leasing fees, renewal fees, and maintenance markup policies with explicit numbers, add that detail now. Confirm each of those posts links back to the relevant geographic service page and to `/brrrr-acquisition-support/`. Strengthening this internal link structure improves topical authority without requiring any new content.
