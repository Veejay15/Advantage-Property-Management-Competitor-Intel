## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

**1. Strengthen the existing Arlington property management page, do not build a new one.**
Advantage already has `/property-management-arlington-tn/`. Reedy just published `/arlington-property-management` with a lastmod of 2026-11-06, the most recently touched page on their site. Audit the Advantage Arlington page now. Add a local market section with current rental rate context, expand the on-page copy to 800+ words, ensure H1 targets "property management Arlington TN," and confirm the page has an internal link from the main `/areas-we-serve/` page. This is the highest-priority response action this week.

**2. Strengthen the existing Germantown property management page, do not build a new one.**
Advantage already has `/property-management-germantown-tn/`. Reedy's `/germantown-property-management` was updated 2026-10-20. Apply the same treatment as Arlington: expand copy, sharpen the H1, add local rental context, and check internal linking. Germantown is a high-income, high-intent market. Thin content on this page is a liability.

**3. Audit the existing `/property-management-fees/` page against Reedy's new `/pricing` page.**
Advantage already has `/property-management-fees/`. Reedy now has a dedicated `/pricing` page, which is a strong conversion tool for owners actively comparing companies. Pull up both pages side by side. If Advantage's fees page is vague, uses ranges without explanation, or lacks a comparison angle, rewrite it. Owners shopping between companies will land on both pages. The more transparent and specific page wins the conversion.

**4. Build a standalone landlord compliance checklist page.**
Reedy published `/landlord-compliance-checklist` on 2026-10-19. Advantage does not have an equivalent page. This is a genuine content gap. A "Memphis Landlord Compliance Checklist" page targeting requirements like habitability standards, notice requirements, security deposit rules, and lead paint disclosure captures top-of-funnel owner traffic and positions Advantage as a credible resource. It also supports internal linking from existing legal and inspection-related blog posts.

**5. Publish a rent-ready checklist page targeting Memphis and suburb-specific searches.**
Reedy published `/rent-ready-turn-checklist` on 2026-10-19. Advantage has `/what-is-a-rent-ready-property-essential-guide-for-new-landlords/` and suburb-specific variants like `/rent-ready-checklist-for-lakeland-landlords-the-exact-steps-before-listing/`. However, Advantage lacks a primary rent-ready checklist page optimized for Memphis and the broader service area. Consider building `/rent-ready-checklist-memphis/` as a hub page that links to the suburb-specific variants, targeting "rent ready checklist Memphis" directly.

**6. Publish a dedicated single-family property management page.**
Reedy now has `/single-family-property-management`. Advantage has `/single-family-homes/` but that URL reads as a listings or property type page rather than a service landing page targeting owners of single-family rentals. Cross-check the content on `/single-family-homes/`. If it is primarily tenant-facing or listing-focused, build a separate `/single-family-property-management/` page targeting owner-intent searches like "single family home property management Memphis." If the existing page already addresses owner intent fully, expand and strengthen it instead.

**7. Evaluate Advantage's owner FAQ and tenant FAQ coverage.**
Reedy now has dedicated `/owner-faqs` and `/resident-faqs` pages. Advantage has `/faq/` as a single combined page. Consider splitting this into separate owner and tenant FAQ pages to better match search intent and improve conversion for each audience. The `/owners/prospective-owners/` and `/tenants/prospective-tenants/` pages may partially serve this function, but a true FAQ page tends to capture more long-tail question-based queries. Review before building to confirm the gap is real.

**8. Monitor Reedy's blog content volume and identify topic gaps for Advantage.**
Reedy's blog library of 200+ posts now covers a wide range of landlord, tenant, and investment topics. Advantage's blog is strong on investment strategy and Memphis market content but should be checked for gaps in tenant-facing informational content (apartment hunting, lease questions, tenant rights) and high-volume landlord how-to content (how to write a lease, how to collect rent, how to handle late payment). These topics drive top-of-funnel traffic that feeds the ownership pipeline over time.
