## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

**1. Audit and strengthen the existing `/property-management-fees/` page immediately.**
Reedy just launched a dedicated `/pricing` page and a supporting blog post (`/blog/property-management-cost-a-comprehensive-breakdown` and `/blog/how-much-do-property-management-companies-charge-rates-explained`). Advantage already has `/property-management-fees/` but needs to confirm it is current, detailed, and structured to answer the same comparison-intent queries Reedy is now targeting. Add a clear fee structure table, address common owner questions about setup fees, leasing fees, and maintenance markups, and consider adding FAQ schema markup to that page.

**2. Expand the existing `/faq/` page to create distinct owner and tenant FAQ sections.**
Reedy now has separate `/owner-faqs` and `/resident-faqs` pages targeting two distinct audiences with high-intent questions. Advantage has `/faq/` but a single page may not be deep enough to compete on segmented queries. Either expand `/faq/` with clearly delineated owner and tenant sections, or evaluate whether splitting into `/faq/owners/` and `/faq/tenants/` is warranted based on your site architecture. Do not build a net-new page if the existing `/faq/` can be restructured to cover both audiences adequately.

**3. Build a dedicated single-family property management service page.**
Reedy launched `/single-family-property-management` as a standalone page. Cross-referencing Advantage's existing pages, there is no equivalent path (Advantage has `/single-family-homes/` which appears to be a property type or listing page, not a service page targeting owners of single-family rentals). Build a service-oriented page at a path such as `/single-family-property-management/` that speaks directly to SFR owners evaluating professional management, highlights Advantage's track record with houses, and targets queries like "single family home property management Memphis."

**4. Build a dedicated multi-family property management service page.**
Reedy launched `/multi-family-property-management`. Advantage has `/duplexes-and-triplexes/` and `/condos-and-townhomes/` but no page that consolidates multi-family management as a service offering under one URL. Build `/multi-family-property-management/` to capture owners of duplexes, triplexes, and small apartment buildings evaluating professional management. This also allows internal linking from the existing duplex and condo pages.

**5. Publish a Memphis-specific landlord legal compliance blog post targeting Tennessee eviction and entry rules.**
Reedy has multiple Memphis-specific legal posts covering landlord entry, lease breaks, eviction fees, and what landlords cannot do in Memphis. Advantage has strong investor and market content but limited Memphis landlord compliance content visible in the existing page list. Produce one authoritative post covering Tennessee landlord-tenant law fundamentals for Memphis rental owners (notice requirements, security deposit rules, eviction process, habitability standards). This directly counters Reedy's legal content cluster without duplicating any existing Advantage page.

**6. Publish a Memphis tenant screening process post.**
Reedy has at least four Memphis-specific posts on screening tenants (`this-is-how-to-screen-tenants-in-memphis-tn`, `how-to-do-a-tenant-criminal-background-check-in-memphis`, `ways-to-find-the-best-tenant-background-check-options-in-memphis`, `landlord-checklist-for-new-tenant-what-memphis-owners-must-do-first`). Advantage has no equivalent page visible in the existing list. A single well-structured post on how Advantage screens tenants in the Memphis market, including criteria, background check process, and income verification standards, would fill this gap and serve both SEO and owner conversion purposes.

**7. Review the `/owners/prospective-owners/` page and align it more directly with the comparison-stage queries Reedy is targeting.**
Reedy's blog cluster on "how to choose a property management company," "questions to ask before hiring," and "are property management services worth the cost" is built to intercept owners still shopping for a manager. Advantage has `/owners/prospective-owners/` but should verify this page directly addresses those comparison-stage concerns. If it currently functions more as a capabilities overview, add a section that speaks to the decision process and links to `/property-management-fees/` and `/faq/`.

**8. Do not build a general investments page at this time.**
Reedy launched `/investments` targeting investor acquisition. Advantage already has substantial investor content at `/why-invest-in-memphis/`, `/memphis-buy-and-hold-investing-guide-long-term-returns/`, `/memphis-real-estate-investment-strategy-buy-hold-brrrr-turnkey/`, and numerous BRRRR and market-specific pages. The gap here is not a missing page but potentially a missing hub. Consider whether a `/real-estate-investing-memphis/` index or resource hub page is warranted to consolidate internal links across the existing investor content and surface it more clearly to owners arriving through investment-intent queries.
