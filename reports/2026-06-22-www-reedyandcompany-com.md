## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

With no new Reedy & Company activity to react to directly, the priority this week is forward-looking. These recommendations address known competitive positioning gaps and content opportunities based on the current state of Advantage's existing page inventory.

1. **Audit and strengthen the `/property-management-fees/` page.** Reedy & Company maintains competitive visibility around fee-related searches. Advantage already has this page, but review it now to confirm it addresses common owner objections clearly, includes a breakdown of what each fee covers, and uses updated 2026 language. Thin or vague fee content loses leads to competitors who are more transparent.

2. **Expand `/owners/prospective-owners/` with a direct response to the "why hire a property manager" question.** Advantage has `/benefits-hiring-property-manager/` and `/property-management-benefits/`, but check whether `/owners/prospective-owners/` itself is substantive or just a navigation page. If it is thin, add specific owner-facing proof points (average days to lease, tenant retention rates, maintenance response times) directly on that page rather than relying on the user to click through.

3. **Build a content cluster around Memphis-area seasonal maintenance topics for the summer leasing season.** Advantage has `/essential-seasonal-maintenance-checklist-for-rental-property-owners/` and `/preparing-rental-properties-for-severe-weather/`. Extend this into a practical summer-specific piece targeting landlords preparing units for turnover in June and July. This is a recurring seasonal search window and a quiet week from Reedy is the right time to publish before they do.

4. **Review internal linking from high-traffic service area pages to related blog content.** Advantage has a strong set of location pages (Bartlett, Cordova, Germantown, Collierville, Lakeland, Millington, etc.) and a growing library of investor and landlord content. Confirm that each location page links to at least one relevant blog post or resource page. Weak internal linking leaves ranking potential on the table even when the content exists.

5. **Confirm that the `/areas-we-serve/` page is current and lists all active service markets.** Advantage has individual pages for markets across Shelby County, DeSoto County, and Fayette County. The hub page should reflect the full footprint, link to every active location page, and be crawled and indexed without canonicalization issues. A quiet competitive week is a good time for a technical spot-check on this page.
