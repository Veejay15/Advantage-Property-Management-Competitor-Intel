## 1. Executive Summary

- Reedy & Company executed a full website migration this week, moving their entire URL structure from a trailing-slash format (e.g., `/blog/post-title/`) to a clean non-trailing-slash format (e.g., `/blog/post-title`). Every blog post, core service page, and site section was affected. This is a significant technical SEO event. If redirects are not properly implemented, Reedy will face widespread ranking losses in the short term. If redirects are clean, the new structure may improve crawl efficiency and signal a broader site overhaul to Google.
- Alongside the URL migration, Reedy launched several new core service and structural pages under the new URL scheme, including dedicated single-family and multi-family property management pages, a standalone pricing page, an owner FAQ page, a resident FAQ page, a portals page, and an investments page. These are high-intent pages targeting owner acquisition and tenant conversion.
- The homepage was also updated this week, suggesting this is a coordinated site redesign rather than a routine update.
- Advantage should monitor Reedy's redirect implementation closely and use this window of potential ranking instability to push content and authority on shared target keywords.

## 5. Recommended Actions for Advantage Property Management

**1. Target Reedy's migration window immediately.**
Reedy has just restructured their entire URL architecture. Google will spend the next two to four weeks re-crawling and re-evaluating their redirects. This is the optimal window to push content on shared keyword targets. Prioritize publishing or updating pages that compete directly with Reedy's high-intent core pages (pricing, owner FAQs, service types). Any ranking gaps created by their redirect chain errors are temporary but exploitable.

**2. Strengthen your existing pricing page to outperform Reedy's new `/pricing` page.**
Advantage already has `/property-management-fees/`. Review and expand this page this week. Add a clear fee comparison table, address common owner objections, include Memphis-specific fee context (management rate, leasing fee, maintenance markup), and ensure the page is internally linked from `/owners/`, `/owners/prospective-owners/`, and the homepage. Reedy's new pricing page will target high-intent owner comparison searches. Your existing page needs to be stronger, not just present.

**3. Expand your owner FAQ page to compete with Reedy's new `/owner-faqs` page.**
Advantage already has `/faq/`. Audit whether this page is segmented by audience (owners vs. tenants) or generic. If it is not owner-specific, add a dedicated owner FAQ section or a new page anchored to prospective owner questions: fees, contracts, tenant screening process, maintenance handling, and what to expect in the first 90 days. Reedy is making a direct play for owner trust with a standalone FAQ. Match and exceed it.

**4. Build a dedicated multi-family property management page.**
Reedy has launched `/multi-family-property-management` as a standalone service page. Advantage has `/duplexes-and-triplexes/` but no clear multi-family property management service page. This is a genuine content gap. Build a page at `/property-management-multi-family/` or `/multi-family-property-management-memphis/` that addresses the specific concerns of multi-family owners: unit turn coordination, rent roll management, common area maintenance, and portfolio-level reporting. Target owner search queries like "multi-family property management Memphis."

**5. Build a dedicated single-family property management page.**
Reedy has launched `/single-family-property-management`. Advantage has `/single-family-homes/` but this appears to be a property type or listings page rather than a service-focused management page. Build a page explicitly targeting "single-family property management Memphis" that speaks to the owner audience, covers leasing, maintenance, tenant screening, and financial reporting for single-family rentals, and links to your relevant service area pages.

**6. Build a dedicated investments page.**
Reedy has `/investments` targeting investor-facing acquisition and property management services. Advantage has strong BRRRR and investor content across many blog posts and individual pages but no clear top-level investor hub. Build `/investments/` or `/real-estate-investing-memphis/` as a hub page that consolidates your existing investor content (BRRRR guides, buy-box criteria, market analysis posts) and positions Advantage as the management partner for Memphis investors. This page should link out to your strongest investor posts and include a clear call to action for investor consultations.

**7. Publish a direct-response blog post targeting "property management companies Memphis" comparison searches.**
Reedy's `/blog/6-key-traits-of-a-top-property-management-firm` and `/blog/navigating-the-best-property-management-companies-in-memphis` are aimed at capturing landlords in the comparison and selection phase. Advantage does not have an equivalent piece positioned from its own perspective. Write a post titled something like "How to Choose a Property Management Company in Memphis: What Actually Matters" that covers evaluation criteria, red flags, and fee transparency. This targets the same high-intent landlord audience Reedy is pursuing with comparison content.

**8. Monitor Reedy's redirect implementation over the next two weeks.**
Check whether `/blog/post-title/` (with trailing slash) properly 301 redirects to `/blog/post-title` (without). If any of their high-ranking posts are returning 404s or soft 404s due to redirect errors, those keyword positions are temporarily vulnerable. Use a crawler or spot-check their top-performing URLs via their blog index. Flag any broken redirects to your team as opportunities for targeted content pushes on those exact topics.
